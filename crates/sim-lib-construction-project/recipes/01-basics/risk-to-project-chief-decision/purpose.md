This modeled recipe traces one current construction risk from its cause and
uncertain event through method-bearing time, exact-amount, and customer
consequences to project-chief attention.

The exposure is derived from current project facts, an accepted schedule
baseline, critical-path joins, and control-graph dependents. Amounts are grouped
only when risk/opportunity kind, scenario, and currency agree; correlated facts
stay annotated and parent/child summaries are never counted twice. The final
row is an attention recommendation. It retains the owner and authority but does
not make or record the project-chief's decision.
