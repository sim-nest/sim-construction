# Closeout to reference admission

This modeled recipe carries one synthetic project from an exact as-of final
economy through typed closeout obligations to an accountable reference-pack
admission.

Commercial facts use `sim-ledger` Amount values and versioned read-only ledger
or office references. Warranty/contact handoff, retention policy, unresolved
work, evidence disposition, and lessons remain separate accepted obligations.
People-development, property, and city-district claims use aggregate charter and
outcome facts, minimal evidence references, consent or confidentiality
clearance, and a named disclosure authority.

The result is an immutable manifest of ids, fact sequences, external references,
as-of date, visibility, and approving decisions. It neither copies source
artifacts nor publishes a reference.
