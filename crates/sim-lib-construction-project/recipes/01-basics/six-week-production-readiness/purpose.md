This recipe shows a production lookahead derived from the same construction
project facts used by gates, supplier handoff, and Gantt schedule impact.

Production readiness is not a second checklist. Released design, permit,
award/handoff, work preparation, risk assessment, method, material, equipment,
staffing, competence, introduction, logistics, inspection plan, and predecessor
work are shared requirements with evidence states, owners, validity windows,
exceptions, and source references. A human commitment is recorded separately so a
weekly reliability review can distinguish a late fact from a bad commitment.
