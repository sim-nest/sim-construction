This modeled recipe proves a ventilation system ready for contractual handover
from explicit commissioning, test, defect, O&M, as-built, training,
certification, authority, customer-acceptance, and remaining-work requirements.
The system spans two areas, so the same leaf evidence rolls into each area
without copying a tree or double-counting inside either report.

Technical, evidence, authority, contractual, occupancy/use, and final completion
remain separate sequence-bound gates. A bounded customer acceptance covers one
remaining-work item for contractual acceptance, but final completion stays
blocked until that item has accepted closure evidence. Documents remain versioned
`ExternalRef`-style references; the recipe creates no document store, issue
tracker, percentage override, or commissioning transport.
