an accepted corrective result. The source item is correlated by stable source,
external id, and version, while the vendor payload, attachments, credential,
and URL remain at the site boundary. The read leaves an effect-ledger reference
and the imported project fact remains reported rather than accepted evidence.

ahead of a critical progress signal. A rejected inspection continues to block
production until the corrective action carries reference-only evidence and an
accountable acceptance.
