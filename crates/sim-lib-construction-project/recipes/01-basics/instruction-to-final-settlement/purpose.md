This modeled recipe carries one stable `ChangeId` from a customer instruction
through scope, time, supplier exposure, customer recovery, quotation, authority,
forecast, execution, settlement, and accountable closure.

Every commercial component uses the exact `sim-ledger::Amount` representation in
the project charter currency. Document values and ledger balances retain source
versions and as-of dates as reference-only evidence. They do not approve a
quotation, post a journal, prove payment, or close the change. Those outcomes
remain explicit authority, settlement, and closure facts.
