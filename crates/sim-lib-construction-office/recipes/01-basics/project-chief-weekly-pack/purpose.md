# Project-chief weekly office pack

Projects one synthetic as-of project snapshot into the existing office `Doc`,
`Sheet`, and `Deck` values, then presents those values through the installed
suite scene. The pack carries its project, sequence and date, accepted
baselines, selected source controls, evidence references, caller-supplied
generation marker, and visibility policy.

Decisions and non-waivable safety or legal blockers lead the weekly cadence.
Evidence exceptions follow before schedule, readiness, procurement, risk,
change, economy, and handover. Every status has a value and explanation, and a
reported mandatory control prevents an accepted aggregate.

The projection filters project facts with the caller's `Cx` capabilities before
constructing any office value. Restricted supplier, commercial, people, and
incident facts therefore cannot affect counts, labels, links, deck content, or
suite metadata. The office values are disposable projections; the construction
fact book remains authoritative.
